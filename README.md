# Baka NPR バカ

**Free stylized rendering toolkit for Blender 5.3 (EEVEE).**
Built for my short film project, *Psycho Chicken / EGGzotics*, and shared so other creators can get to their look faster.

![Baka NPR]

## Features
- **Two looks, one click** – *Grey* (sketch paper, painted blue shadows, grit) and *White* (clean paper, flat lavender shadows). Each keeps its own lighting and settings.
- **Line art that knows distance** – tapered ink lines, bold up close, thin pencil far away, never disappearing. Hidden objects drop their lines in the viewport.
- **BG Sun** – background objects keep a clear light and shadow side, while your key and rim lights stay on the hero.
- **Shadow focus & tiers** – shadows strongest around your main character; separate primary / secondary / background treatment.
- **Distance Haze** – a soft horizon tint for depth.
- **Mood presets** – 12 cinematic day/night lighting moods.
- **Toonify** – turn realistic scenes and imported assets into the toon look, with original materials kept.
- **Character tools** – feathers & tail controls, rim light, glow.
- **F12 = final quality** – shadows, samples and output set for the final render, then your working settings are restored.

## Install
1. Download the latest `baka_npr_v1.x.x.zip` from [Releases](../../releases).
2. In Blender: **Edit → Preferences → Get Extensions → ⌄ → Install from Disk…**
3. Open the **Baka** tab in the 3D viewport sidebar (N).

Requires **Blender 5.3+**. EEVEE is the main engine; Cycles support is experimental.
